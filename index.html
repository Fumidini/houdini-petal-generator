VEX walkthroughs for each Wrangle node in a Houdini tool that builds cherry blossom petals. The network flows from top to bottom; pick a node to read about.

circleCircle SOP
輪郭のもとになる円。The circle the outline starts from.
basePoint Wrangle
Base - 花びらの輪郭
Base - the Petal Outline
円の点を、花びらの輪郭（XZ平面）に並べ替える。幅と長さのカーブを、付け根・最大幅・先端の 3 か所で決める。
Re-places the circle’s points into a flat petal outline on the XZ plane, shaped by width and length curves at the root, widest point and tip.

resample1Resample SOP
点を均等な間隔に打ち直す。Re-spaces the points evenly.
fix_curveuDetail Wrangle
Fix Curveu - curveu の振り直し
Fix Curveu - Re-assigning curveu
Resample で変わった curveu を、付け根 0 → 先端 0.5 → 付け根 1 にそろえ直し、先端の点を記録する。
Rebuilds curveu as root 0 → tip 0.5 → root 1 after Resample, and saves the tip point for later nodes.
全 6 ステップ / 6 steps
noisePoint Wrangle
Noise - 輪郭のゆらぎ
Noise - Wobbling the Outline
輪郭を左右非対称にして、大小 2 つのノイズで外側・内側にゆらす。
Makes the outline asymmetric, then pushes it in and out with a large and a small noise.

Notch - 先端の切れ込み
Notch - the Tip Cleft
先端の点を引っ込めて V 字を作り、まわりの肩を外に広げる。桜の花びららしい先端の形。
Pulls the tip point in to make a V and spreads the shoulders outward: the classic cherry-petal tip.
